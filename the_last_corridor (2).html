Engine & Graphics Notes

1. First-Person Perspective & Multi-Touch Camera

Uses a THREE.PerspectiveCamera(75, aspect, 0.1, 42) at human eye height (1.65m). Supports both desktop PointerLock mouse-look and multi-touch drag-to-look with simultaneous analog thumbstick movement.

2. Procedural PBR Materials (Albedo, Normal & Roughness)

Synthesizes tileable CanvasTexture maps at startup: weathered ashlar masonry, tangent-space normal maps derived from finite-difference height gradients $(\partial h/\partial x, \partial h/\partial y)$, and roughness maps for porous stone and wrought iron.

3. Dynamic Torch Attenuation & Spatial Audio

Combines exponential distance fog (THREE.FogExp2) with a shadow-casting point light whose radius scales with remaining fuel, alongside procedural Web Audio synthesis for stone footsteps and gate mechanisms.

4. Axis-Separated Circle-vs-AABB Collision

Resolves X and Z displacements independently against a 0.44m bounding cylinder so the player slides smoothly along walls without camera clipping.
